How WohnIQ works

01 You describe it Budget, rooms, area, commute, quiet or lively: in your own words, English or German.
02 We rank and check Each flat is scored on fit, its rent compared with the Mietspiegel, and screened for scam patterns.
03 You see why Every result explains itself: what matches, what doesn’t, and what to ask the landlord.
